Why this lands on you

Your supplier has no filing obligation. You do.

Under EUDR the party that first places goods on the EU market is the operator, and the operator carries primary legal responsibility. A supplier promise is not a defence.

WHO CARRIES WHAT
ORIGINIndian factory. Provides documentation on request. No EUDR filing duty of its own — the burden does not start here.
YOUEU importer — the operator. Collects the evidence, assesses the risk, files the due diligence statement, carries the liability.
ONWARDDownstream buyers. Reference your statement number rather than assembling their own evidence — and will ask you for it.
WHAT NON-COMPLIANCE COSTS

Penalties reach up to 4% of EU annual turnover, alongside seizure of goods and exclusion from the EU market. And if a supplier's timber turns out to be unevidenced, the remedy is a different supplier or a different timber source — a production-cycle decision, not a paperwork one.

EUDR EVIDENCE FILE · STRUCTUREPDF + INDEXED ANNEXES
§ 01Supplier profile. Legal entity, premises verified in person, manufacturer or trader established.
§ 02Timber documentation register. Transit permits, purchase records and species declarations, photographed and matched to material on the floor.
§ 03Supply chain map. Named yards, traders and intermediaries between harvest and factory.
§ 04Origin & geolocation record. Plot coordinates where the chain allows them to be established, with method and confidence stated.
§ 05Species verification. Declared species checked against what is physically in the batch.
§ 06Batch linkage. Wood sources tied to your PO, cartons and container — not the supplier in general.
§ 07Gap register. What could not be evidenced, why, and what would close it. Usually the most useful section.

Questions

EUDR evidence — FAQ

Can my Indian supplier handle EUDR for me?

No. Non-EU suppliers have no filing obligation under EUDR — their role is to hand you documentation so you can complete your own due diligence. Legal responsibility sits with the operator placing the product on the EU market. If they can't produce the evidence, the exposure stays with you.

What if the supplier can't produce plot coordinates?

That is the common outcome on a first review, and it is exactly what you need to know. Much Indian hardwood reaches the factory through timber yards rather than direct from a plantation, so the chain often stops at a purchase record. We document how far it goes, state where it breaks, and set out what would close the gap.

Can you certify my supplier as EUDR compliant?

No, and nobody can. EUDR operates no certification scheme. What exists is evidence, a risk assessment and a statement you file. We collect and verify the evidence; the statement and the judgement stay yours.

Can this run alongside a pre-shipment inspection?

Yes, and it is cheaper that way. If we are already attending the factory for an inspection, the documentation audit can run on the same visit. Mention it at scoping and we will price it combined.
