Questions

Packaging & transit validation — FAQ

What is ISTA 3A and why does it matter for furniture?

ISTA 3A is a standardised test protocol that simulates the drops, impacts and handling of a parcel-delivery network — the exact journey a flat-pack or e-commerce furniture carton takes. Running the sequence before shipment tells you whether the packaging will survive the trip, while the fix still costs a respec rather than a wave of returns.

How is this different from a pre-shipment inspection?

A pre-shipment inspection checks the quality of the furniture. Packaging & transit validation checks the box and the journey — whether the carton, protection and kit will get a perfect product to the customer intact. For parcel-shipped and e-commerce orders, that is where the returns actually come from.

What happens if the packaging fails the drop test?

We photograph the failure, specify exactly what needs to change — board grade, corner protection, void fill — and re-test the revised packaging before the container ships. You sail on a packaging spec that has survived the sequence, not one that hasn't been tried.

Do you check Amazon and marketplace packaging rules?

Yes. We verify the carton, labelling and kit against the parcel-network expectations of Amazon-class fulfilment centres before the goods are sealed, so you are not surcharged or rejected at intake after the container has already landed.
