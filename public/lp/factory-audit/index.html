What an audit surfaces

The risks an inspection on one order can't see

An inspection proves a single shipment was right. An audit tells you whether the factory can build your volume, to standard, order after order — before you commit to it.

AUD-01 · CAPACITY

A factory that can't build your volume

Claimed monthly output that the workforce, shifts and machine hours don't support. The over-promise surfaces as slipped dates and cut corners once your order is live.

AUD-02 · SUBCONTRACTING

Work quietly leaving the building

Carving, finishing or upholstery sent to unlisted units you never approved and can't see. Quality drifts the moment it's out of the factory's control.

AUD-03 · QC SYSTEM

No system — just a final look

The eight sections

Every section scored and photographed

  • ✓A-01 Profile & legitimacy — ownership, history, export record and documents, building on any prior supplier verification.
  • ✓A-02 Production process — machining, joinery, assembly and finishing flow, with bottlenecks and risk points mapped.
  • ✓A-03 Material control — timber sourcing, kiln capacity and cycle records, moisture management and storage.
  • ✓A-04 Quality control system — in-process checkpoints, final QC, defect recording, golden-sample handling and calibration.
  • ✓A-05 Real capacity — workforce, shifts and machine hours against claimed monthly output — the over-promise check.
  • ✓A-06 Subcontracting map — which processes leave the building, to whom, and how that work is controlled.
  • ✓A-07 Workforce & safety — working conditions, safety equipment and basic social-compliance observations.
  • ✓A-08 Compliance readiness — ability to meet destination requirements: fire labels, formaldehyde docs, timber-legality paperwork.

Questions

Factory audit — FAQ

How is an audit different from an inspection?

An inspection checks the goods on one order, usually at around 80% packed. An audit assesses the factory itself — whether it has the process, quality system, real capacity and compliance readiness to build your volume to standard, order after order. Different questions, which is why buyers scaling a supplier book the audit first and inspections after.

How long does it take, and when do I get the report?

One full day on site for a typical Jodhpur or Jaipur factory, with the scored report delivered within 3 working days. Larger export houses or multi-site suppliers may need two days.

What do I actually receive?

A report scoring all eight sections (A-01→A-08), a photographed record, a clear verdict — programme-ready, approve with a corrective action plan, or do not scale — and a recommended inspection plan for the orders that follow.

What happens if the factory scores conditional?

You get a corrective action plan with deadlines against the specific sections that failed. When the factory says it has closed the gaps, we re-audit only those sections at a reduced fee, so you commit the order against a resolved report rather than a promise.

Can you run our own vendor audit protocol?

Yes. Retail chains and brands can substitute their own audit protocol — we execute it on the ground and report in your format. Same auditor, your checklist.

How much notice do you need?

Same day is often possible in Jodhpur and Jaipur, 24 hours in Delhi NCR, and 48–72 hours in Saharanpur, Moradabad and Kolkata. Tell us early and we will hold the slot — rescheduling costs you nothing.
