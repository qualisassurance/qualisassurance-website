What a pre-shipment inspection catches

The defects that reject containers and trigger claims

We've photographed and classified every failure pattern in the Indian furniture trade. Here are the ones that cost buyers the most.

DEF-011 · WOOD · MOISTURE

Unseasoned timber cracking

Sheesham shipped above 12% moisture splits when it meets central heating in the US or EU. The single largest claim category.

CRITICAL
DEF-032 · STRUCTURE · JOINT

Joint & frame failure

Subcontracted assembly with undersized tenons or missing corner blocks — invisible until a customer sits down.

CRITICAL
DEF-024 · FINISH · HUMIDITY

Finish bloom in transit

Lacquer cured in monsoon humidity clouds and blushes inside a hot container before it reaches you.

MAJOR
DEF-047 · QUANTITY · COUNT

Short shipment

Cartons counted at the door, not the invoice. Orders arrive 5–20% short with no recourse after payment.
