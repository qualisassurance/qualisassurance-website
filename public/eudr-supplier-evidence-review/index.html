WHY THE TIMING MATTERS

EUDR applies to large and medium operators from 30 December 2026, and to micro and small enterprises from 30 June 2027. If a supplier's timber turns out to be unevidenced, the remedy is a different supplier or a different timber source — and that is a production-cycle decision, not a paperwork one.

The deliverable

EUDR EVIDENCE FILE · STRUCTUREPDF + INDEXED ANNEXES
§ 01Supplier profile. Legal entity, IEC and GST status, premises verified in person, manufacturer or trader established.
§ 02Timber documentation register. Transit permits, purchase records and species declarations — photographed, dated and matched to the material on the floor.
§ 03Supply chain map. Named yards, traders and intermediaries between harvest and factory, with premises checks where they are reachable.
§ 04Origin and geolocation record. Plot coordinates where the chain allows them to be established, with the method and confidence stated for each.
§ 05Species verification. Declared species checked against what is physically present in the batch at inspection.
§ 06Batch linkage. Documented wood sources tied to your PO, cartons and container — so the file matches the shipment, not the supplier in general.
§ 07Gap register. What could not be evidenced, why, and what would be needed to close it. The most useful section in the document.

Scope

What this is, and what it is not

We are an independent inspection firm, paid by buyers and never by factories. An evidence file assembled by someone the factory pays is not evidence — which is the whole reason this service exists.

Included

  • ✓On-site documentation audit at the supplier's premises.
  • ✓Upstream tracing through yards and traders (Level 2 and above).
  • ✓Geolocation capture where the chain permits it to be established.
  • ✓Species checked against physical stock, not just the declaration.
  • ✓An honest gap register, and a call to walk you through it.

Not included

  • —Filing your due diligence statement. That obligation sits with you as operator and cannot be delegated.
  • —EUDR certification. No such scheme exists — treat anyone offering it with suspicion.
  • —Legal advice or a compliance opinion. We document facts; your counsel interprets them.
  • —Laboratory species testing. Available on referral if a claim needs lab confirmation.
  • —Any guarantee of a clean result. If the evidence isn't there, the file says so.

Questions

EUDR evidence review — FAQ

What if my supplier can't produce plot coordinates?

That is the common outcome on first review, and it is exactly what you need to know. Much Indian hardwood reaches the factory through timber yards rather than direct from a plantation, so the chain often stops at a purchase record. We document how far it goes, state where it breaks, and set out what would be needed to close the gap — so you can decide whether to work on the chain or change the source.

Can you do this alongside a pre-shipment inspection?

Yes, and it is usually cheaper that way. If we are already attending the factory for a PSI or during-production inspection, the documentation audit can run on the same visit. Tell us at scoping and we will price it combined.

Is one review enough, or is this ongoing?

Depends on your buying pattern. A single supplier with stable sourcing may need one thorough review plus periodic refresh. If you are shipping continuously from several factories with mixed timber lots, batch linkage has to be maintained per shipment — that is what the programme level covers.

Does this replace supplier verification?

No, it sits on top of it. Supplier verification establishes whether you are dealing with a real manufacturer. The evidence review establishes whether the timber behind that manufacturer can be documented. If you are onboarding a new supplier, run verification first.

Who signs the report?

The inspector who did the work. Not a company stamp, and not an anonymous freelancer — the same person walks you through the gap register on the debrief call.
